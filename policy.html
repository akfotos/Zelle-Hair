The Policies

Transparent terms for shipping, returns and the rituals that keep your hair luminous.

Shipping Returns & Exchanges Care Guide Privacy

01
Shipping

Every Zelle order is prepared by hand, wrapped in signature black lacquer packaging and dispatched with tracking.

Processing

Orders are processed within 1–2 working days. Custom and pre-order pieces are confirmed by our concierge with an estimated dispatch date.

Delivery

  • UK standard: 2–4 working days — complimentary on orders over £150.
  • UK next working day: available on eligible orders placed before 1pm, calculated at checkout.
  • International: 5–12 working days. Local duties and import taxes may apply.

Tracking

A tracking link is emailed the moment your piece leaves the atelier. Signature is required on delivery for orders over £250.

02
Returns & Exchanges

We stand behind every strand. If a piece isn't right, we'll make it right.

14-Day Window

Unworn, unaltered hair in its original packaging may be returned or exchanged within 14 days of delivery. Hygiene seals must be intact.

What can't be returned

  • Hair that has been worn, washed, dyed, bleached or heat-styled.
  • Lace that has been cut or customised.
  • Custom-made or personalised pieces, unless faulty.

Exchanges

Texture or length exchange is complimentary — we cover return shipping on your first exchange. Start a request via our contact page.

03
Care Guide

Treat Zelle hair as you would your own — gently, and with intention.

Wash Ritual

  • Detangle from ends to roots before washing.
  • Use sulphate-free shampoo in lukewarm water, stroking downward — never scrub.
  • Deep-condition monthly; rinse cool to seal the cuticle.

Styling

  • Air-dry whenever possible. Apply heat protectant before hot tools (max 350°F / 175°C).
  • Finish with two drops of Argan Gloss Ritual Serum for mirror shine.

Rest

Sleep in a satin bonnet or on a silk pillowcase. Braid or loosely tie long textures overnight.

04
Privacy

In accordance with UK GDPR, we collect only the information needed to fulfil your order and improve your experience — never sold, never shared beyond our delivery and payment partners. You may request access, correction or deletion of your data at any time via our contact page.
